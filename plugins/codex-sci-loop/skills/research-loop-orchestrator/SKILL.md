# Research Loop Orchestrator

Before starting a scientific phase, classify the work and require the matching
project-local skills and artifacts:

- research core: task tree, invariants, deterministic verification;
- simulation: bounded experiments, independent cross-checks, provenance;
- data analysis: data contracts, provenance, independent cross-checks;
- manuscript: source audit, consistency audit, result provenance;
- full research: all applicable safeguards above.

Record required skills in the phase objective and verify their artifacts during
audit. Missing required scientific evidence results in `REWORK` or `BLOCKED`,
never `ACCEPTED`.
